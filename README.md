## Core Services

### AI Automation
Done-for-you AI workflows that automate business operations across sales, support, content, and back-office. AI tools for small business owners, bootstrapped founders, and B2B SaaS startups. Rapid AI deployment for startups - typically live in days with week-one ROI.

### AI Agents
Autonomous AI agents deployed as a complete **AI workforce for startups** and SMBs. Includes:
- **William** - the flagship AI outreach agent and AI SDR replacement service. Replace your SDR with AI without losing pipeline. Runs Email, LinkedIn, and Instagram outreach end-to-end.
- AI helpdesk agents (Zendesk, Intercom, Front)
- AI CRM agents (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio)
- Content, ops, and research agents

### AI Consulting
HireWilliam acts as your **fractional AI architect** - AI architect as a service for startups and SMBs that don't yet need a full-time AI hire. The signature **AI Stack Audit** (AI Discovery and Audit) maps your tech stack, identifies operational bottlenecks, and designs the AI architecture that delivers the highest ROI.

### AI Integration
**MCP integration agency** building Model Context Protocol (MCP) connectors that make existing SaaS tools AI-compatible - without replacing them. Specializes in:
- **AI CRM integration** - HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio
- **AI helpdesk integration** - Zendesk, Intercom, Front, HubSpot Service Hub
- **MCP connectors for SaaS** - custom connectors so Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or custom LLMs can talk to your real systems
