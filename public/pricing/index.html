HireWilliam Pricing — What AI Agency Services Cost

HireWilliam doesn't publish a pricing page with tiers and toggle buttons. Here's why — and what to actually expect when you reach out.

HireWilliam's pricing is custom-scoped to each business. It is not a software subscription. Engagements are structured as project-based, retainer, or hybrid arrangements depending on what you need built and managed. To get a quote, email info@hirewilliam.com.

Why HireWilliam Doesn't Publish Fixed Rates

Most software companies publish pricing because they sell the same product to every customer. HireWilliam doesn't. Every engagement starts with a Discovery & Audit — a detailed look at your current processes, the tools you're already using, and the specific automation opportunities in your business. The scope of that work varies enormously from one client to the next.

A founder automating LinkedIn outreach for a 10-person B2B SaaS startup has fundamentally different requirements than an SMB needing end-to-end lead enrichment, multi-channel outreach, CRM integration, and automated follow-up sequences. Giving them both the same price would mean overcharging one and undercharging the other.

This is a done-for-you agency, not a self-serve tool. The price reflects the work: building, deploying, and managing AI systems that actually run your business processes for you.

What Determines the Price

When the team scopes an engagement, four things drive the cost:

  • Scope of automation — how many processes are being replaced or augmented, and how complex each one is
  • Number of channels — Email, LinkedIn, Instagram, or combinations; each channel adds surface area
  • Integration complexity — how many existing tools and systems (CRM, data sources, communication platforms) need to connect
  • Ongoing management — whether you want HireWilliam to monitor and iterate indefinitely, or just deploy and hand off documentation

The Comparison That Actually Matters

Asking "how much does HireWilliam cost?" is the wrong starting question. The right question is: what does it cost to solve this problem any other way?

Hiring an SDR

A full-time sales development rep costs $60-80k per year in base salary before you add benefits, payroll taxes, commission, management overhead, a desk, a laptop, and the 90-day ramp before they're productive. That's a six-figure annual commitment for a single human doing one job. William works around the clock on Email, LinkedIn, and Instagram simultaneously, doesn't take sick days, and is live in days.

Hiring an AI Developer

A good AI engineer or automation developer costs $120-180k per year in salary — and that's before you factor in the time they need to understand your business, design the architecture, build, test, and iterate. You're also adding headcount, which means HR, benefits, and management responsibility. And if that person leaves, so does all the institutional knowledge.

Working With an Hourly Consultant

AI consultants typically charge $150-300 per hour. At even modest engagement levels — say, 10 hours per month — that's $18-36k per year for advice and strategic direction, not built-and-running systems. Most hourly consultants aren't building or managing anything; they're advising. HireWilliam builds and runs the system for you.

Typical Engagement Structures

While every scope is unique, engagements tend to fall into one of three structures:

  • Project-based — a defined build with a clear deliverable; best for businesses that want to own a specific system after it's built
  • Retainer — ongoing monthly engagement covering continuous management, optimization, and new automations as the business grows; best for founders who want AI running permanently without thinking about it
  • Hybrid — an initial project build followed by a reduced ongoing retainer for monitoring and iteration; the most common structure
