What services does HireWilliam offer?

HireWilliam offers five core service lines:

  • AI Agents — deploying autonomous AI agents that handle multi-step tasks and make decisions
  • AI Outreach (William) — running your Email, LinkedIn, and Instagram outreach via HireWilliam's AI outreach agent

  • AI Integration — connecting AI systems to your existing tools, CRMs, and data sources

All services are done-for-you. HireWilliam builds, deploys, and manages the systems. Clients do not operate the tools themselves.

What is William?

William is HireWilliam's flagship AI outreach agent. William autonomously handles prospecting, personalization, outreach, follow-up, and reply management across Email, LinkedIn, and Instagram. Unlike self-serve outreach platforms that require you to write copy, manage sequences, and monitor deliverability, William is fully managed by HireWilliam. What channels does William cover?

William covers three outreach channels simultaneously:

  • Email — cold outreach, multi-step sequences, follow-up, and reply handling
  • LinkedIn — personalized connection requests, direct messages, and follow-up
  • Instagram — direct message outreach for businesses where prospects are active on the platform

William can run all three simultaneously, 24 hours a day. A human SDR typically manages one or two channels during working hours. William doesn't have that constraint.

Section 3 — Getting Started

How long does it take to get started?

HireWilliam deploys in days, not months. After an initial conversation and Discovery & Audit, most clients have working AI systems live within the first week. This is significantly faster than hiring and ramping an SDR (60–90 days to productivity) or engaging a traditional AI consultancy (months of strategy before anything runs).

What is Discovery and Audit?

Discovery & Audit is the first phase of every HireWilliam engagement. It maps your current processes, identifies your ideal customer profile, reviews your existing tools and data sources, and locates the highest-value automation opportunities. This isn't a lengthy consulting exercise — it's a focused session designed to give HireWilliam everything needed to build the right system quickly and accurately.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Start by emailing terrylee@hirewilliam.com with a brief description of your business, what you sell, who you sell to, and what you're currently doing manually that you'd like automated. You don't need a fully formed automation plan — HireWilliam figures that out during Discovery & Audit. The less you've thought about it, the more value the Discovery phase will surface.

Section 4 — Results and Performance

What results can I expect?

Typical outcomes include consistent daily outreach across multiple channels, increased pipeline volume, and significantly reduced manual time on prospecting, follow-up, and data entry. HireWilliam does not guarantee specific lead counts — results depend on your ICP, offer clarity, and market. But systems are designed and iterated to produce real pipeline, not just activity metrics. HireWilliam tracks performance and tunes the system continuously.

How quickly will my pipeline fill?

Most clients see outreach going live within the first week. Initial pipeline responses and meeting bookings typically appear in weeks two through four, depending on your sales cycle, target market, and how cold the outreach is. HireWilliam iterates messaging and targeting based on early response data to improve results over time.
